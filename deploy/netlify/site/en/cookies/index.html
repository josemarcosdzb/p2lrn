Cookies policy

Information about cookies and local storage used by P2LRN to provide the functions of the website.

Last update: October 30, 2026

On this page Cookies utilizadasSummaryAlmacenamiento localOffline operation and cacheThird partiesConsentSet upManagement in the browser

1. Cookies used

P2LRN does not currently use its own cookies for analytics, advertising or marketing.

The website uses local storage and caching only when necessary for specific functions, such as the viewer or basic contact form protection.

The website does use browser storage other than cookies for specific functions. For this reason, this policy separately explains local storage and the cache used by the viewer.

3. Local storage

The contact form can temporarily save the time of the last submission in the browser to avoid immediate repetitions. This information remains on the user's device.

4. Offline operation and cache

The viewer can persist course resources locally to enhance certain offline features. This cache is used for functional purposes, not for advertising or profiling.

5. Third party services

The website uses infrastructure providers for hosting and receiving messages. These providers may generate technical logs necessary for security and operation in accordance with their own policies and conditions.

6. Consent

P2LRN does not currently load optional analytics, advertising or marketing categories that require their own consent panel. If optional technologies are added in the future, this policy and consent controls will be updated before they are activated.

7. Configure cookies and storage

There are currently no optional categories to enable or disable from P2LRN. The “Set Cookies” link in the footer returns to this section to explain the actual status instead of showing functionless switches.

8. Management from the browser

The user can delete the data stored by P2LRN from the privacy or site data settings of their browser. Doing so will create the cache and local preferences again when they are needed again.

Official reference on cookie consent: AEPD cookie guide.
