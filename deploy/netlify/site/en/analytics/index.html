Understanding the demonstration

This panel displays sample data to illustrate how courses, learner progress, attempts and results appear in Analytics. It does not represent real students or official grades from an institution.

If you have a .p2lrn-result file from a course delivered offline, use Open result to review it here. Results collected from offline activity are informative until an authorized learning platform accepts them through its own process.

To connect Analytics to real institutional data, see the Analytics integration guide. The integrating platform is responsible for learner identity, access permissions and the authorized source of its results.

Explore the sample data step by step

  1. Open Overview to review the totals and become familiar with the indicators. The values on this page are fictional and intended only to demonstrate the dashboard.
  2. In Learners, select a sample learner to inspect progress by course, attempts and recorded activity. Use the filters to find particular records.
  3. In Courses, compare learners' progress. Starting a course is not the same as completing it, and recorded activity alone does not prove that an assessment has been passed.
  4. In Grades, distinguish official grades supplied by an authorised source from informative results. Do not automatically turn an offline delivery score into an official grade.

Offline results, permissions and privacy

If you have a .p2lrn-result file from an authorised course delivery, choose Open result and select the learner's returned file. Check which course and delivery the file belongs to before interpreting its attempts. Do not open unknown results or process personal data without your institution's authorisation.

This demonstration does not manage real accounts or enrolments, does not submit grades to an LMS gradebook and does not replace your institution's tracking system. Real use requires the institution to configure identity, permissions and the provenance of records. The Analytics integration guide explains the technical setup.
