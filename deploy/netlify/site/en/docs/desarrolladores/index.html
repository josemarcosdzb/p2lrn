Skills and MCP: different tools for different tasks

A Skill supplies instructions and, where supported, scripts to create or verify course files. Installing one does not grant an assistant access to your files. MCP connects an authorized client to specific tools, directories and operations, subject to your permissions.

What to verify before going live

  1. Open the same package in Player and complete the activities in a real browser.
  2. Authorize each learner, institution and course on the Host; do not trust IDs supplied by the browser.
  3. Keep learner results separate from official grades until the LMS confirms them.
  4. Use CDN/npm versions that are actually published and verified together.
