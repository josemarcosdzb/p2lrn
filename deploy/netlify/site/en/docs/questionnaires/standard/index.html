Interactive quizzes with answers and scoring

Present the questionnaire as a conventional evaluation. The learner responds with controls adapted to the type of question: options, text, order, relate, classify or fill in gaps.

Answer and grade a questionnaire

Use it when you want to mix question types or when direct evaluation is more important than a game mechanic.

Write questions and define how they are marked

A quiz can bring several question types together in one assessment. For each question, you choose the response type, accepted solution and score.

Activity setup in the editor

  1. Choose the activityOpen your course and its unit. In Add content, select Assessment (Evaluación) → Questionnaire (Cuestionario).
  2. Write the instructionsEnter a complete question, such as 'What causes water to evaporate?'.
  3. Set the answersUse the Type field to choose single choice, multiple choice, true/false, text or another supported response. Add options and mark the correct one.
  4. Review the settingsCheck Points, feedback and presentation settings. For a fill-in-the-blanks question with draggable words and distractors, see the worked example. Use Add question to include more questions.
  5. Try the learner activityComplete a quiz with correct and incorrect answers in Learner preview, then check the total score.

Questionnaire result after submission

How the learner responds: Read each question and use the option or input that corresponds to its type. For example, the third planet from the Sun is Earth. Submit the quiz to obtain a score. The Spanish screenshot shows a final result of 26/26 points, not the controls used to answer.

Accessibility, scoring and results

Interaction and accessibility

The controls are HTML forms and have associated tags. It does not depend on dragging or a graphical scene.

Completion and scores

When you submit the assessment, the quiz updates its status and reports score and completion when the correction is available.

To save the result on your platform, see Events and Host Adapter.

Practical example: create an interactive online quiz

To assess a digital safety unit, prepare three questions about passwords, suspicious links and account protection. Add them to one Quiz activity in Studio rather than creating three separate activities.

  1. Configure the questions.Choose supported question types and check choices and solutions. Keep instructions separate for each question.
  2. Review the learner journey.Enable one question at a time if appropriate. Check that Previous and Next let learners reach every question before submitting the complete assessment.
  3. Control when feedback appears.Do not show correct answers while learners are still answering unless the teacher has explicitly enabled that behavior. Check the overall result on completion.
  4. Export and test.Download the .p2lrn and check your own course in the viewer to verify behavior on mobile and desktop.

Player scores do not automatically become official LMS grades. If you need enrollment and grades, connect identity and persistence through an authorized Host Adapter autorizado.
