PRACTICE / EDUCATIONAL ACTIVITIES

Create interactive educational activities through play

Not every exercise should be a test or a 3D scene. You can use word searches, crosswords, word wheels, memory, matching and classification inside a P2LRN course. Choose the activity based on what learners need to practice.

Compare available gamesOpen the viewer

STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE

Choose an activity based on the skill you want to develop

  1. 01

    Recognize words

    Use word searches, crosswords or word wheels to reinforce vocabulary. Prepare clear clues and check accents and accepted answers.

  2. 02

    Match concepts

    Memory and matching activities connect definitions, images or examples. Review how correct associations are shown and how learners can change answers.

  3. 03

    Order and classify

    Use sequences for procedures and categories for classification. Design clear alternatives without revealing the solution early.

  4. 04

    Test real interaction

    Play the package in Player and test mouse, keyboard, scrolling and error messages. Learners should recognize feedback without relying on color alone.
