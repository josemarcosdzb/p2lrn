STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE

Four steps to prepare a complete assessment

  1. 01

    Define what is assessed

    Write several questions linked to the lesson objective. Avoid ambiguous answers and decide whether you need an assessment or a practice activity.

  2. 02

    Author the quiz

    In Studio, open a lesson, add an assessment and configure its questions. Keep instructions, answers and teaching content separate.

  3. 03

    Review the results

    Check when feedback and scores appear. Navigate all questions before completion to verify that learners do not receive solutions prematurely.

  4. 04

    Test the real course

    Export the .p2lrn package, open it in Player and test navigation, keyboard access and mobile scrolling through the final question.

FREQUENT QUESTION

Do I need an LMS to create quizzes?

No. You can create them in Studio and play the file with Player. Official grades, enrollments and identity require an authorized integration with an LMS.
