Privacy Policy

Information about the data that is processed when using the contact form and the services necessary for the website to function.

Last update: October 30, 2026

1. Controller and contact

Communications sent through this website are managed within the framework of the P2LRN project. For questions about privacy or exercise of rights, use the contact page e indica Privacy in the matter.

2. Processed data

The form requests name, email address, subject and message. The route from which the query is sent and the technical information strictly necessary to prevent abuse and provide the service can also be recorded.

3. Purpose

The data is used to receive, organize and respond to inquiries about P2LRN, its tools, licenses and integrations. The form does not subscribe the user to commercial communications or create an advertising profile.

4. Legal basis

The processing is carried out to respond to the request initiated by the contacting person and, where appropriate, to manage measures prior to a contractual relationship or legitimate interests linked to the security and operation of the site.

5. Service providers

The website uses infrastructure providers to host the site and receive form messages. In the current deployment, web hosting and Supabase services are used. These providers may process technical data in accordance with their own terms and applicable agreements.

6. Security

The form applies input validations and access controls to prevent messages from being publicly exposed. Measures to limit abuse also apply. No system can guarantee zero risk, so measures are reviewed as the service evolves.

7. Data retention

Messages are kept for the time necessary to manage the query and meet any legal or security obligations that may be applicable. When they are no longer necessary, they must be deleted or anonymized in accordance with the service's preservation criteria.

8. Rights

When the RGPD is applicable, you can request access, rectification, deletion, limitation, opposition or portability in the cases provided by law. Send the request from Contact specifying Privacy in the matter. You can also complain to the competent control authority.

9. Automated decision-making

No profiling code or automated decisions with legal effects on the user have been observed in the contact flow.

10. Updates

This policy should be reviewed if forms, providers, database permissions, purposes or storage technologies change.
